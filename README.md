## 👋 Hello

I'm **Sam P** — a developer who's been at this since computers showed up in my life in **'93**. Three decades in, I build fewer things and finish more of them: open, sovereign tools at the intersection of **Bitcoin**, **Nostr**, and **free software**. I care about craft, privacy, and work that outlives the hype cycle.

### ✨ Original Work
*By commit activity.*

| # | Project | Live Demo | What it does |
|---|---------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | [ns](https://github.com/psam21/ns) | [nostr.ltd](https://nostr.ltd) | **Nostr relay (Go).** A self-hosted Nostr relay built on a Shugur fork plus a Blossom server — the infrastructure powering nostr.ltd and the backbone for the Nostr work above. |
| 2 | 🔒 [Epoch Shield](https://github.com/psam21/nipees) | [epochshield.com](https://epochshield.com) | **One key, infinite identities.** A zero-knowledge, client-side encrypted identity system built on the NIP-EES spec — each passphrase derives a separate, compartmentalized identity (messages, groups, vault, mail) over Nostr. Crypto is **scrypt + NIP-44**, fully open source (AGPL). Coercion-resistant: a wrong passphrase opens a convincing decoy identity. Free forever for individuals. |
| 3 | [nip-ees-core](https://github.com/psam21/nip-ees-core) | — | **NIP-EEs core library & spec.** The reference implementation and specification behind Epoch Shield — encrypted, epoch-scoped event sharing for Nostr (private groups and messages that stay confidential and expire cleanly). |
| 4 | 🔒 [haiku](https://haiku-hour.vercel.app) | [haiku-hour.vercel.app](https://haiku-hour.vercel.app) | **A new haiku every hour.** A generative poetry engine publishing 283 haikus across 50+ categories — small, daily acts of making that keep the joy in building. |
| 5 | [NostrForNomads](https://github.com/psam21/NostrForNomads) | [nostr.co.in](https://nostr.co.in) | **Nostr for Nomads.** A Nostr-native hub for travelers and communities — decentralized social for people on the move, live at nostr.co.in. |
| 6 | [cb](https://github.com/psam21/cb) | [culturebridge.vercel.app](https://culturebridge.vercel.app/) | **Culture Bridge.** Preserving heritage & tradition by tying culture to Bitcoin + Lightning + Nostr — a living archive that rewards and sustains cultural work. |
| 7 | [biffes26](https://github.com/psam21/biffes26) | [biffes26.vercel.app](https://biffes26.vercel.app) | **⭐ Most-starred original project.** A Progressive Web App for the 17th Bengaluru International Film Festival (Jan 30 – Feb 6, 2026) — film discovery across 13 categories, an 8-day multi-venue schedule, watchlists with shareable 6-char codes, and a multi-source ratings aggregator (IMDb/RT/Metacritic/Letterboxd). Built with Next.js 16 + React 19 + Upstash Redis; local-first with cloud sync. |
| 8 | [ipl26](https://github.com/psam21/ipl26) | [ipl26.vercel.app](https://ipl26.vercel.app) | **IPL 2026 — "The Grid".** The definitive view of all ten franchises: auction purse spent, top buys, squad composition (domestic/overseas), and title/top-4 probabilities — interactive and live at ipl26.vercel.app. |
| 9 | [nrpc_workshop](https://github.com/psam21/nrpc_workshop) | — | **Nostr RPC workshop.** Hands-on material for building on Nostr's RPC surface — notes and code from a working session. |
| 10 | [strc-price-panel](https://github.com/psam21/strc-price-panel) | — | **STRC price panel for the desktop.** A lightweight panel that surfaces live STRC market prices right on your desktop. |
